300 (2007)

Zack Snyder
117 minutes
Action, Exploitation, Fantasy, War

English
Rating:
3.0 / 5.0
★★★★★
★★★★★

300 possesses a thoroughly defined mythology through audible narration, otherworldly enemies, and the values found in our Spartan characters.

The film is not short on visual flourishes, such as when Leonidas kicks the messenger into the well, the soldiers being forced off the cliff, and the spear that makes a god-king bleed. One incredible sequence follows a tracking shot of Leonidas, savagely dispatching his enemies; it's made better by the fact that it also follows the trajectory of his spear throw and then returns to Leonidas, continuing the carnage for an absurd amount of time.

There is a highly selective use of color, seemingly with an intent to highlight the blood and Spartan cloaks. Music is used to set the mood, with electric, symphonic, choral, and percussive choices all made carefully to maximize drama.

Iconic lines throughout, such as "this is Sparta", "then we will fight in the shade", "The world will know that free men stood against a tyrant, that few stood against many, and before this battle was over, even a god-king can bleed" give the film a distinct dialogue identity. It seeks to establish itself as somewhat "era appropriate" but ultimately aims for modern accessibility.

Subtlety is not in the film's vocabulary, overstating every scene, usually to the overall benefit of the film. 300's primary limitations are packaged with its form. It doesn't have much to offer beyond some cool action and high levels of testosterone. Even the non-action sequences ultimately serve to lionize Leonidas, even off the battlefield. While the violence in this film is impressive and effective, it is often substituted for other fundamental elements. The mixed dialogue keeps the film accessible, but occasionally breaks immersion when the characters offer commentary that is overly modern.

Action-packed, hyper stylized, but rather empty headed, 300 is a solid pick for those seeking thrills above story.
