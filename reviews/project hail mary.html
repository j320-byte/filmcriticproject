Project Hail Mary (2026)

Phil Lord, Christopher Miller
157 minutes
Science Fiction

English
Rating:
4.5 / 5.0
★★★★★
★★★★★

The premise presented in Project Hail Mary is a tried-and-true trope of science fiction: You woke up in space. What the hell? But beyond its science fiction adornment, a charming and funny story is present about the resilience of the human spirit and friendship.

Our stakes are high, with all of humanity in peril. Appropriately, the film remains above political posturing and instead depicts a unified effort to save the planet.

The journey is harrowing but never horrifying. Cinematography captures the scope of their task, such as when they must carefully align their ships as well as when they descend to collect samples. Music works in the film's favor too, matching the mood of our journey in a nuanced way; a good example is when Rocky is attempting to send a package to Grace, played by Ryan Gosling, for the first time. There is a playfulness to the music as Grace realizes that Rocky has, correctly, identified him as being incompetent.

The relationship between Grace and Rocky is technically complex and narratively excellent. Together they can do what they could never do alone: save their home worlds. They both commit themselves to tasks the other is incapable of; Rocky makes a 5 km chain with his bare "hands" for their experiment. Grace, through gentle explanation, is able to tell Rocky why his crew died.

Rock handles the concrete; his vision is literally based on differentiating surfaces topographically; flat images provide him no visual information. His material strengths are significant. Grace handles the abstract; he attacks their problems scientifically and hides the fact that he is destined to die in space, an emotionally complex maneuver meant to spare Rocky sadness.

Rocky is incredibly brought to life, with exceptional note given to the scenes where he is operated as a puppet. Seeing an actual, tactile object allows the viewer to attach to the character visually and emotionally. Overall visual design for Rocky and his technology is fascinating. Rocky himself is, well, a rock; but he has little gemstones embedded in his body that add visual flair. His ship, both exterior and interior, has a unique design and appears as if composed of needles and thread.

Grace's identity as a teacher is paramount to the success of the mission and the film. He is given a task no one anticipated: deciphering an alien language, teaching science to a rock, and facilitating communication with a creature that, from a human perspective, was well below the communicative abilities of even a child. The reviewer is skeptical any of the other selected astronaut candidates could have pulled this off. Grace cites his lack of bravery as a deficiency; he does not opt into his mission, claiming to not have the bravery gene. One of the astronauts laughs at him, "It is not a gene. You just need to find someone willing to be brave for."

Ryan Gosling depicts charm and Grace with a great deal of conviction. His chemistry with his non-human counterpart is made impressive by the fact that Gosling performed double duty in terms of making his performance physical and expressive. Gosling's best moment comes when Rocky reveals he can help him make it home; after a display of stoicism, Gosling becomes a complex mixture of overwhelmed, thankful, relieved, and left sitting in disbelief.

The film drives its point home when Grace realizes that his friend, now very far away, is in peril; without hesitation, he finds and saves Rocky. Their friendship, even across many miles, was unbreakable.

This also leads to a delightful conclusion for Grace's arc. After Grace returns with Rocky to his homeworld and is lavishly accommodated by Rocky's people, it is revealed that Grace has not only survived and received a happy ending, but he has also returned to the classroom. Chattering rock children exclaim excitedly at the sight of their teacher and a genuine smile spreads across Grace's face.

After viewing Project Hail Mary, the reviewer can only agree with Rocky: "Amaze! Amaze! Amaze!"
