Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981)

Steven Spielberg
115 minutes
Action, Adventure

English
Rating:
5.0 / 5.0
★★★★★
★★★★★

From the moment Raiders of the Lost Ark starts, the audience finds itself transported to remote Peru with a rugged adventurer who, fearless of death, plunders a lost ancient temple. Even after the opening sequence, there is a distinct lived-in quality to the film implying there were many stories already told before this and the audience is familiar with all of them.

Harrison Ford is Indiana Jones, without dispute. He conveys surly frustration, heavy charm, and deep intellectualism as interchangeable parts of the character, thus bringing Indiana to life. His charm is especially apparent when on screen with Karen Allen, playing his romantic opposite, Marion. Their alternation between fighting and tenderness creates a satisfying contradiction.

The film maintains a respectful relationship with the source of its mythology: Christian scripture. Indy even uses a comically large and ancient Bible to illustrate some of his points early in the film. Appropriate to scripture, it is established that the Ark is an artifact of massive significance and power. Indiana even assures Marion during the film's climax to keep her eyes shut, showing an understanding of the Biblical might on display and the implications of gazing upon it.

The visuals of the climactic scene align with the severity of our villain’s sins as their faces melt away satisfyingly. Spielberg’s methods were so effective here that they overlap into reality, leaving the audience wondering how he possibly could have gotten away with liquifying his own actors.

As a globe-trotting film, it was important that each location furthered the feeling of adventure. Every waystation is fantastic and interesting, but the chase is afoot and our characters never stand still for long. From the opening jungle, to the bustling paths of Cairo, and to the final arid island, every setting is perfectly fit to the scene.

With an exceptional story and a daring sense of adventure, this film has generated fans across generations and attained iconic status. Raiders of the Lost Ark is a definitive piece of cinema and an absolute must-watch.
